# Reflection

For me among the three cloud platforms, AWS impressed me the most because of its wide range of services and flexibility. It can support both small startups and large companies.

All three platforms share similarities such as compute, storage, networking, and security features. However, each has its own strengths like Azure for Microsoft systems and GCP for AI.

Business requirements are important when choosing a cloud platform. The best choice depends on the needs of the company, not just popularity.

Also I learned how to analyze cloud platforms and recommend solutions based on real scenarios. I also improved my research and documentation skills.

My GitHub portfolio is now more organized and professional after completing this activity. Out of the three major providers evaluated, Amazon Web Services stood out to me as the most impressive platform overall, primarily due to its massive catalog of features, overall maturity, and unmatched technical adaptability. AWS offers a highly versatile environment that easily scales to support fast-paced early startups building lightweight web applications as well as large enterprise organizations managing complex, mission-critical global infrastructures.

While AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform share core capabilities like scalable computing power, object storage, virtual networking, and enterprise security, each platform excels in distinct areas. Azure offers seamless integration for enterprise environments heavily reliant on Microsoft systems and hybrid cloud configurations, while GCP shines in advanced machine learning pipelines, artificial intelligence tools, and large-scale data analytics.

finally choosing a cloud provider should never be based on brand popularity alone; it must be driven entirely by a company's specific technical requirements, budget constraints, and long-term operational goals.
